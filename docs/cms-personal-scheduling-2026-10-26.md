# CMS entry and personal chapter schedules

Henry requested IS/CS and the other learning levels at the top of CMS, removal of the fixed 2026–2027 study calendar, and flexible chapter dates, reminders and visible progress.

- The existing IS/CS, AS and MS practice entry points now precede chapter cards. Existing question filters remain unchanged.
- The fixed annual plan is removed from the learner page; its source data is preserved.
- Each module has optional start/target dates, a one-time reminder (Beijing time), edit/clear controls and progress on CMS home, inside the module and My Learning.
- Progress counts unique completed core sections, extension lessons, attempted questions and checked outcomes. It is not an accuracy or qualification score. Dismiss does not count as answering a question.
- Device-only test storage migrates lazily without removing earlier learning records. Clearing a plan preserves progress. No login or cross-device service was added.
- Due reminders appear throughout the open App until acknowledged. They stop for fully completed modules; changing the reminder time rearms them. A saved reminder can be exported as an ICS calendar event with a chapter deep link. Imported events are managed separately in the user's calendar; closing the App does not itself send system push notifications.
- Tests cover invalid dates, flexible future years, Beijing/UTC boundaries, persistence, migration, independent modules, reminder lifecycle, calendar exports and progress integrity.
