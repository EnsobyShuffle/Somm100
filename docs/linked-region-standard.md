# Linked region dossiers

Grape varieties and regions are canonical, independently addressable records. The approved Aligoté and Bouzeron formats remain the presentation standard. French and Italian regional content lives in `data/region-dossiers.json`, with a lightweight `region-index.json` for routing, filters and search. Existing Bouzeron and Spanish content keeps its original routes and research.

English and Chinese remain separate. Grape names are canonical and untranslated. A named appellation opens its exact dossier; ambiguous aliases must never select a record arbitrarily. Administrative and geographical overviews are not legal appellations. Source-specific role notes retain the distinction between authorized grapes, experimental plantings and heritage associations. Bidirectional links do not imply that a partial list is the entire legal encépagement.

Each factual section cites its sources. Editorial service suggestions are identified. Effective vintages and dated changes are retained when legislation changes. Producer references are linked externally unless the precise producer-and-wine record already exists in the database.

Review uses separate foundation (SOMM100 / IS / CS, 10) and advanced (AS / MS, 20) question pools, hidden answers and per-region browser-local marks. A dossier without an authored review bank does not advertise one. These are independently authored study questions.

`linked-country-audit.json` records the imported scope, unresolved research and identity corrections. Italy is not labeled as covering every national registration or every appellation. Research batches are retained with the Site source for traceability and recovery.
